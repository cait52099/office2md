4.3 Fundamental safety measures for normal operation 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The machine/system must only be operated by authorised persons 
who are trained on it, know the operation manual and can work in ac-
cordance therewith! 
Prior to switching on the machine, verify and ensure that  
 
only authorised persons are within the work area of the machine  
 
no one can be injured during start-up of the machine! 
Verify the machine before each production start for visible damage 
and ensure that it is only operated in a proper condition! Any defects 
discovered must be reported immediately to a superior! 
Before starting production, remove material/objects that are not re-
quired for production away from the work area of the machine! 
Before starting production, verify and ensure that all safety devices are 
working properly! 
The user must not disable the safety devices on his own authority. 
symex does not assume any warranty obligation for damage incurred 
as a result. 
When entering the target values, the permissible target values indi-
cated on the rating plate and in the flow chart must be observed! 
These must not be exceeded under any circumstances. 
The mixing vessel can be used with overpressure, vacuum, high tem-
peratures and utilizing the pumping capacity of the homogenizer. 
Whenever opening manually or externally controlled valves with a free 
outlet, it must be ensured that nobody is standing in the flow direction 
of the opened pipes. 
All heating-related components and piping may have high surface 
temperatures. 
Furthermore, high temperatures prevail in way of non-insulated sur-
faces of the heated mixing vessel. 
Operating pressures should always be selected at a lower rate than 
the maximum permitted pressures. Otherwise there is the risk that any 
existing safety devices will respond. 
Never reach into the agitator vessel while the agitator is operating. 
Do not hold any tools, measuring instruments or other objects into the 
mixing vessel. 
Do not touch any rotating agitator parts. 
Please also refer to OEM documentation.

7.1.4 Induction systems 
 
For explosion protected systems, the operator must ensure that the system 
and the inducted substances (powder, gases and liquids) are inert (rendered 
unreactive). No foreign substances, which may lead to sparking, may be in-
ducted. 
 
The machine can be equipped with the following induction systems: 
 
1) Dead space free induction valve on the homogenizer 
Controlled by a disk valve, the inducted product is placed directly before the tool 
set of the homogenizer and then pumped via the long loop (recirculation line) into 
the upper part of the vessel.  
This version is used very often for the production of gel on neutralised Carbopol 
basis. Such a gel can be produced without air inclusion within a few minutes. 
A special type of application is the 'direct induction system', by which the sub-
stances are inducted directly into the shear gap of the tool set through a dia-
phragm valve or a disk valve. In this way, the hot-cold method can be imple-
mented perfectly. 
Note: see also, 7.1.8 Homogenizer a.) ad b.) 
 
2) Bottom induction 
A cavity-free piston valve with a relatively large nominal width will be used, which 
is ideally suitable for inducting large quantities of powder. The bottom induction 
method is used, for example, for the production of toothpaste. During induction, 
the agitator operates at high speed to rapidly disperse the powder in the vessel. 
 
3) Induction into the recirculation line 
A flap valve is welded particularly close to the recirculation line in the long loop 
(or the short loop, as the case may be). If the homogenizer is running, the prod-
uct is sucked into the recirculation line and pumped into the upper part (or the 
lower part, as the case may be) of the vessel.  
 
 Air inclusions are extracted by the vacuum pump. 
 
4) Induction into the vessel (above the product level)  
The induction connection lies above the vessel flange and slopes towards the 
vessel. Liquid products can be inducted. Small quantities of air inclusions are ex-
tracted by the vacuum pump.

Supply of solid matter 
 
The homogenizer is designed for homogenising and pumping of liquids with 
or without solids content. 
Through faulty operation during homogenisation/pumping of dispersions, se-
vere damage may be caused to the homogenizer, in particular to the tool set 
and the mechanical seal.  
Therefore it is imperative to observe the following:  
 
1. In case of non-melting solids, it is imperative to make sure that these 
solids are only fed into the homogenizer after the vessel has been first 
filled with liquid at least up to the level of the short loop (the pump 
housing and tool set must be flooded).  
 
2. In case of melting solids (waxes), the system must be heated to at 
least 80°C before starting to feed the solids into the system. 
For this purpose, the homogenizer must be operated in short loop for 
some time with a hot liquid. 
 
 
 
Examples for faulty operation 
 
1. Damage event - 'Burning of solids' 
Without liquid, undue friction may cause the product to heat up sub-
stantially/burn and wear/destruction of the rotor and stator (shear rotor 
and pump rotor). 
 
2. Damage event - 'Blocking, clogging' 
In the production step 'Melting of waxes', the fluid in the tool set of the 
homogenizer solidifies when the substance is fed in and temporary 
standstill of the homogenizer; this generates adhesion between the ro-
tor and stator.  
This means that when the homogenizer starts, the drive unit and the 
tool set overheats. Damage to the sealing and bearing unit, specifically 
the mechanical seal and the counter ring, is then likely. 
Likewise, the medium can solidify in the short loop, block the loop and 
overload the homogenizer. 
 
In both cases, an inspection by the symex service is imperative!

10 Starting the machine 
 
10.1 Safety principles 
 
- 
The safety instructions described in the documentation must be observed to avoid per-
ilous injury of persons as well as damage to property and to the system/machine. 
 
- 
The machine may be commissioned only by qualified persons observing the safety re-
minders. 
 
- 
Ensure that only authorised persons remain in the work area of the machine and that 
no other persons not involved are at risk from the operation of the machine. 
 
- 
Incorrectly wired connections may destroy the electrical/electronic components. The 
machine shall only be used for its specified purpose. 
 
- 
Observe the safety instructions of the manufacturer (OEM documentation). 
 
- 
Do not fail to read the Chapter 'General safety reminders'. 
 
- 
Take note of the proper behaviour in case of an incident before starting the machine. 
 
 
 
10.2  Commissioning 
 
- 
Before starting the machine for the first time, check if all transport locking devices, tools 
and foreign parts have been removed from the machine. 
 
- 
Establish the supply and disposal connections. 
 
- 
Check the protection measures against electrostatic discharges (grounding of all com-
ponents, use of suitable hoses etc.) 
 
- 
Enable/check all safety devices 
- Protective covers of machine parts and electrical equipment 
- Functioning of work platforms/ladders (if any) 
- Functioning of first aid equipment (eye rinsing devices etc., if available) 
 
- 
Verify the proper functioning of the following system components: 
- pneumatic system 
- supply media (water/wastewater, steam/condensate, compressed air, etc.) 
- lubricant cycles (sealing liquid cycle homogenizer/agitator) 
- heating/cooling components
