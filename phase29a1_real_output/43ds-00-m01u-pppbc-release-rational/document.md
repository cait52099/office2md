# Executive Summary

43DS is a water/silicone system formulated with 1.25% KF-6105 and 1.5% KSG-210 DF as emulsifiers. Which are specifically chosen to impart a rapid water breaking sensation. The system incorporates a high internal phase exceeding 80%, and its viscosity is strongly influenced by the processing conditions—homogenization speed and duration—which directly affect the particle size distribution. Smaller particle sizes contribute to higher viscosity. Symex has the challenge in maintaining long loops and operating within the viscosity range. While, M4E is capable of effectively balancing viscosity and recirculation.

The product has an annual production volume of 200 kilograms and is produced at Oevel
